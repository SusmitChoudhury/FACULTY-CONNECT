## Detailed Checkpoint Mapping to INT335 Guidelines

### 1. `CP2_Empathy_and_User_Research.pdf`
- **Methodology:** Dual-track qualitative inquiry across 35 surveyed students, 8 in-depth interviews, and 6 ethnographic observation sessions.
- **AEIOU Framework:** Complete Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects, and Users breakdown in Blocks 34, 36, and 38.
- **Qualitative Evidence:** Transcripts from Aman C. (K22DF), Priya S. (K22DF), Rohan P. (K22GF), Dr. Rajesh Sharma (Cabin 34-601), and Dr. Harpreet Kaur (Cabin 38-402).
- **Empathy Maps:** Student Empathy Map and Faculty Empathy Map covering Says, Thinks, Does, and Feels quadrants with verbatim quotes.

### 2. `CP3_Problem_Definition.pdf`
- **7 Thematic Finding Clusters:** Faculty availability unpredictability, excessive waiting/transit friction, rampant repetition, chaotic channels, absence of bookable slots, social anxiety, and faculty cognitive burnout.
- **User Personas:** Detailed archetypes for *Rohan Patel* (Student) and *Dr. Rajesh Sharma* (Faculty Lead).
- **Current Journey Maps:** 5-stage student and faculty trajectories with plotted emotional valence curves.
- **POV Statements:** Strict Stanford d.school format (User + Need + Insight) free of predetermined software solutions.
- **Selected Main HMW Question:** *"How might we make academic consultation and doubt resolution outside lectures predictable, timely, and organized for students without increasing faculty workload through repeated queries?"*

### 3. `CP4_Ideation_and_Selection.pdf`
- **Three Ideation Methods:** Brainwriting 6-3-5 (adapted for 4 members), Crazy 8s (32 rapid sketches), and SCAMPER morphological manipulation.
- **36 Ideas Log:** Complete numbered table with titles, descriptions, ideation methods, and team initials (`AS`, `KH`, `AT`, `SC`).
- **Affinity Clustering:** 6 strategic solution groups ranging from physical hardware boards to intelligent knowledge engines.
- **Pre-Weighted Screening Matrix:** 6 candidate concepts scored against User Value (25%), Faculty Acceptance (20%), Feasibility (20%), Effort (15%), Scalability (10%), and Research Fit (10%).
- **Selection & Rejection Analysis:** Defensible selection of *Faculty Connect* (Score: 4.56/5.0) and post-mortem analysis of rejected concepts.

### 4. `CP5_Prototype_Readiness.pdf`
- **Prototyping Evolution:** Version 1 (Low-Fi Paper Wireframes) vs Version 2 (High-Fi React 19/Flask PWA).
- **Visual User Flows:** Dual-track flowchart detailing decision branches, duplicate detection pathways, and error states.
- **Narrative Storyboard:** 6-panel contrast between the current corridor struggle and the smooth Faculty Connect experience.
- **Figma Design System:** Verified palette (`#15370D`, `#FCF9EA`, `#F2842F`, `#000000`), 10 master screen frame catalog, and live URL.
- **Neutral Test Plan:** "Show, Don't Tell" non-intrusive observation protocol with 4 unassisted task scenarios for students and faculty.

### 5. `CP6_Testing_and_Improvements.pdf`
- **First-Round Usability Logs:** Individual testing records for 5 real participants (P1-P4 students, P5 faculty Dr. Rajesh Sharma).
- **Structured Feedback Matrix:** 6-column matrix classifying issues into Serious, Medium, and Small.
- **Key Identified Defects & Fixes:**
  - *Serious:* Modal drawer stuck open after faculty answer; fixed by direct transition to `Resolved` and auto-closing drawer.
  - *Medium:* Need for direct 1-click AI generation on dashboard cards without opening deep drawers; implemented.
  - *Medium:* AI answers required deep syllabus grounding; upgraded with academic synthesizer (Diamond problem, Round Robin math).
  - *Small:* Enlarged mobile touch targets for "+1" button to 48px; added permanent team footer.
- **Second-Round Retesting:** Validated with 2 fresh participants (P6 and P7) confirming 100% resolution of all previous friction points.

### 6. `CP7_Final_Showcase_and_Report.pdf`

- **Appendix A:** Approved CP1 Registration Sheet details.
- **Appendix B:** Official Annexure C Individual Contribution Statements with specific tasks, evidence locations, and signature blocks for all 4 team members.
- **Appendix C:** 12-Slide Final Presentation Sequence designed for the 10-minute evaluation presentation.
- **Appendix D:** Step-by-Step Live Demonstration Script for evaluating the production deployment.
- **Appendix E:** Individual 2-Minute Viva Preparation Guides for Aryan Singh, Kaya Hamir Reddy, Aditya Tiwari, and Susmit Choudhury.
